Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information Buddhi Score handles when you use buddhiscore.in, and why. Buddhi Score is operated by OWNER_FULL_NAME, OWNER_CITY, India.

In short: you do not create an account, your test answers stay in your own browser, and we never see your card or UPI details.

Information that stays on your device

  • First name and age group that you type before the test.
  • Your answers, timings and result, so your report can be shown again if the page reloads.
  • Your sound setting.

These are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to our server. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Payments

When you buy the full report, the payment is handled by Razorpay. You enter your payment and contact details on Razorpay's checkout, not on our pages. We do not receive or store card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords. We can see the order ID, payment ID, amount, status and the contact details you gave Razorpay, in our Razorpay account, and we use them only to confirm payment, give support, process refunds and meet tax and legal record-keeping duties. Razorpay's own privacy policy applies to its processing.

Shared links

If you choose to share your result, the link itself contains your first name, age group and scores. Anyone who has the link can see that summary, and it can be forwarded. Share a child's result only if you are comfortable with that.

Hosting and other services

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, processes technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver pages and keep them secure. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the checkout script from Razorpay, so those services receive your IP address when the page or checkout loads.

What we do not do

  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No profiling of children.

Children

Children under 18 should take the test with a parent or guardian's consent. We do not ask children for contact details, and a child's test data stays on the device as described above.

Your choices

You can clear your saved report from your browser at any time. To ask about payment records we hold, to request correction or deletion where the law allows, or to raise a complaint, write to the grievance contact on our contact page. We aim to reply within 7 working days.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
